FAQ

Questions worth asking

Is this on the Google Play Store?

Not yet — we're still finishing testing before submitting. Until then, "Download the APK" gives you a direct, real build straight from our GitHub releases. It's the same app you'll eventually find on Play.

Wait, is it safe to install an APK from outside the Play Store?

Yes, as long as it comes from a source you trust — this one is built automatically from the exact source code in our public GitHub repo, so anyone can inspect it. Your phone will show an "install blocked" warning the first time; you'll need to allow installs from the app you used to open the file (Files, Chrome, etc.) in Settings.

Do I really have to do push-ups to turn off the alarm?

Yes — that's the whole point. Set your rep target (3 by default), and the alarm keeps ringing until your camera confirms you've completed them with full range of motion.

Does the app record or upload video of me?

No. All pose detection runs entirely on your device using Google's ML Kit. No video or images ever leave your phone.

What if I can't physically do push-ups?

Pick squats or jumping jacks instead — both are built in and work the same way, just tracking different joints. More exercise types are still on the way.

Which phones does it work on?

Any Android phone running Android 8.0 (API 26) or later with a front-facing camera.

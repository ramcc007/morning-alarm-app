Frequently asked questions

Do I really have to do push-ups to turn off the alarm?

Yes — that's the whole point. Set your rep target (3 is the default), and the alarm keeps ringing until your camera confirms you've completed them with full range of motion.

Does the app record or upload video of me?

No. All pose detection runs entirely on your device using Google's ML Kit Pose Detection. No video or images ever leave your phone.

What if I can't physically do push-ups?

We're actively building additional exercise types (squats, jumping jacks, and more) so everyone can find a movement that works for them. Push-ups ship first.

What happens after the free trial?

After 3 days, your subscription renews automatically at ₹50/month unless you cancel first in Google Play's subscription settings. You're never charged during the trial itself.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Subscriptions are billed and managed entirely through Google Play — cancel anytime from the Play Store app → Payments & subscriptions on your phone.

Which Android phones does it work on?

Any phone running Android 8.0 (API 26) or later with a front-facing camera.

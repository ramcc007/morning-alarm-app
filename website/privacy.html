Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

What MorningAlarm does with your camera

MorningAlarm uses your device's front-facing camera solely to detect body pose (via Google's on-device ML Kit Pose Detection) while you're completing your exercise to dismiss an alarm. This processing happens entirely on your device. MorningAlarm does not record, store, transmit, or upload any camera image, video, or derived body-pose data to us or to any third party, at any time.

What information we do collect

We collect the minimum needed to run the app and your subscription:

  • Alarm settings you create (times, repeat days, exercise targets) — stored only on your device.
  • Subscription status and purchase transactions, handled by Google via Play Billing. We receive confirmation of your subscription status but never your payment details, which Google manages directly.
  • Basic crash/diagnostic data, if you opt in via Android's standard Play Store data sharing setting.

Notifications

MorningAlarm schedules local notifications on your device to ring your alarms. These are generated and delivered entirely on-device; we do not operate a push notification server and do not see when your alarms fire.

Third parties

Subscription billing is processed by Google under Google's own privacy policy. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Data retention & deletion

Alarm data lives in the app's local storage on your device and is deleted automatically if you delete the app. To stop your subscription and any associated billing data held by Google, cancel it from the Play Store app → Payments & subscriptions on your phone.

Children's privacy

MorningAlarm is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for material changes, notify users in-app.
